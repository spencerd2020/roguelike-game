Mistfang Karst

An original monsoon jungle. Limestone towers stand out of the mist, rainforest giants hold the canopy up on plank buttresses, banyans drop roots into pillars, and bamboo, banana and elephant-ear leaves fill everything below. A jade river turns a bamboo water wheel that feeds flooded terraces. The village is seeded, from a hamlet to a small town. It has stilt houses up out of the floods, each one hollow, furnished and full of things you can pick up. There is a jetty where the dugouts land the fish, and a timber yard where the village saws its ironwood.
