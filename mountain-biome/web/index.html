Vanehold Pass

An original mountain biome, high above a sea of cloud. Peaks of banded slate, rose and cream stone stepped into strata ledges, a gold alpine meadow, a glacial tarn, a gorge crossed by a rope bridge, and a village of wind-wheel houses. Every model was generated in code and baked in Blender with painted, palette-only textures lit from above. Each building's inside matches its outside.
