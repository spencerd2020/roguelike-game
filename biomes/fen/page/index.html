Ambermere Fen

An original wetland biome. Tea-dark amber water, sphagnum hummocks in rust, ochre and lime, sedge and cotton-grass, bladder lilies, pitcher plants and sundews, and pale tallow trees carrying flat tiers of rust-gold needles. The fen folk cut peat from the raised bog and build round houses of peat blocks on wattle-ringed islands in the mere, joined by log boardwalks. Every model is generated in code and baked in Blender, and every texture is painted from that bake in the fen's own palette.
